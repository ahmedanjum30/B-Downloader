Not connected
Add the extension to your browser.
What it does
  • Downloads you start in the browser go to BDownloader.
  • Right-click a link and choose Download with BDownloader.
  • Videos, YouTube too, get a Download this video bar.
How to add it
Firefox opens the add-on's page: click Add to Firefox.
Firefox didn't open? Open this page in Firefox:
https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/bdownloader/
It shows as connected here once the browser has talked to BDownloader.
